# The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched — GrandmaGram

GrandmaGram is our entry for The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched: a social network exclusively for grandmothers to rate each other's grandchildren, complete with a premium tier that lets you boost your grandson to the top of the family leaderboard. It is pitched with total founder confidence, because the worst ideas always are. The program writes the one-sentence pitch to `/output/entry.txt`.
